Step 01 — Eligibility

Service requirements, plainly.

You qualify if you meet one of these minimums. The Certificate of Eligibility (COE) is the official document — your lender can usually pull it same-day.

★

Active Duty

90 continuous days of active service during wartime, or 181 days during peacetime.

⚐

National Guard / Reserves

6 years of service, or 90 days of active-duty service.

✚

Veterans

Honorable discharge after meeting the active-duty or guard/reserves minimums.

♡

Surviving Spouses

Un-remarried spouses of veterans who died in service or from service-connected disability.

Step 02 — Funding Fee

A one-time fee in place of monthly mortgage insurance.

It's how the VA keeps the program self-sustaining — and it's usually rolled into your loan, so you never write a check.

Step 03 — Credit

The VA sets no minimum. Lenders do.

Most lenders set their own overlay around 580–620. But the VA itself cares more about residual income, your overall pattern, and how recent any setbacks were.

The VA doesn't have a minimum credit score. Anyone who tells you "you need a 620" is quoting their lender's rule — not the program's.

Step 04 — DTI & Residual Income

The VA does something no other loan does.

It pairs DTI with residual income — the cash left over each month after taxes, housing, debts, and basic living costs. A 50%+ DTI can still pass with strong residual income.

%

DTI Benchmark

41% is the VA's guideline — not a hard cap. Higher is fine if residual income is solid.

$

Residual Income

Gross income minus federal/state/SS taxes minus housing/debts/living. Must clear regional minimums.

⌂

Family Size Matters

Required residual income scales with household size and region (NE, MW, S, W).

+

Compensating Factors

Strong reserves, long job tenure, low debt — all push higher DTIs through underwriting.

Step 06 — Appraisal

The VA appraisal & the Tidewater process.

If the appraiser thinks the home will come in under contract, you get a heads-up — and a chance to defend the price.

1

Lender orders

Appraisal ordered through the VA portal once contract is in.

2

Tidewater notice

Appraiser flags potential low value before completing the report.

3

Comps submitted

Listing agent has 48 hours to send supporting comps.

4

Notice of Value

Final value issued. If low: renegotiate, pay the gap, or walk.
